# Month-by-Month Timeline & Checklist

Wedding: **Saturday, September 4, 2027**
Today (start): **May 19, 2026**

Each phase below corresponds to roughly how far out from the wedding you
are. Check items off as you go. Items marked **[CO]** are
Colorado-specific.

---

## Phase 1 — Now through July 2026 (15–14 months out): Foundation

This is the most important phase. Everything that follows depends on it.

- [ ] **Pick your "top 3" priorities.** Sit down together for an hour and
      write the three things that matter most (photos, food, music,
      venue view, guest experience, etc.). Tape it to the fridge.
- [ ] **Set a budget number.** A single total dollar figure you're both
      comfortable with. Be honest about who's contributing what (you
      two, parents, etc.). See `02-budget.md`.
- [ ] **Draft a guest count range.** Not a list yet — just a range like
      "80–110." This drives venue size and 60% of your budget.
      - Low (50–80): intimate, more per-guest spend possible
      - Mid (80–130): the most common Front Range wedding size
      - Large (130+): narrows venue options significantly in the foothills
- [ ] **Decide on a wedding style.** Pick one or two adjectives:
      "rustic mountain," "elegant garden," "modern industrial,"
      "casual outdoor." This guides every aesthetic choice later.
- [ ] **Decide on a vibe for the day** — formal (black-tie / cocktail
      attire) vs. semi-formal (suits / sundresses) vs. casual (jeans
      welcome). Mountain weddings skew semi-formal.
- [ ] **Start the venue search.** See `03-colorado-venues.md`. Goal:
      tour 4–6 venues and have one signed by **end of July 2026**.
- [ ] **Decide if you want a planner.** If full-service, hire now (they
      help with the venue search). If month-of only, hire in early 2027.
- [ ] **Create a shared planning email** (e.g. `yournames.wedding@gmail.com`).
      Use it for all vendor correspondence. Forward to both of you.
- [ ] **Start a shared spreadsheet** for the guest list (name, email,
      address, RSVP, dietary, +1 status, side, party group).
- [ ] **Engagement photos** (optional but recommended) — most photographers
      include them. Useful for save-the-dates and gets you comfortable
      being photographed together.

## Phase 2 — August through October 2026 (13–11 months out): Big vendors

With the venue locked, you can now book the rest of the big-ticket vendors.
The good ones in Denver/Boulder/foothills book up fast for Labor Day weekend.

- [ ] **Book the photographer.** Single biggest decision after the venue.
      Look at 3+ full galleries, not just highlight reels. Ask for a
      complete recent wedding gallery to see how they handle low light
      and rain.
- [ ] **Book the videographer** (if you want one — see budget tradeoffs).
- [ ] **Book the caterer.** Some venues require their in-house caterer
      or have a preferred list — clarify before booking.
- [ ] **Book the band or DJ.** DJs are typically $2–3.5k, bands $5–12k.
- [ ] **Lock the officiant.** Friend, religious officiant, or
      professional. **[CO]** Colorado allows self-solemnization — you
      can legally marry yourselves with no officiant at all (more in
      `05-guest-experience.md`).
- [ ] **Start dress / suit shopping.** Wedding dresses take 6–9 months
      to order plus 2–3 months for alterations. Order by **December 2026**
      to be safe. Suits can wait until ~6 months out.
- [ ] **Reserve hotel room blocks** at 2–3 hotels near the venue. **[CO]**
      Labor Day weekend is peak Denver tourism — book blocks NOW, not
      later. See `05-guest-experience.md`.
- [ ] **Send save-the-dates.** Standard timing is 6–8 months out, but
      for a Labor Day weekend with mostly out-of-town guests, send
      **9–10 months out** (November–December 2026) so people can book
      flights.
- [ ] **Build a wedding website** (Zola, The Knot, Withjoy are all
      free and good). Include: date, location, hotel blocks, altitude
      warning, dress code, travel info, registry.

## Phase 3 — November 2026 through January 2027 (10–8 months out): Aesthetic

- [ ] **Book the florist.** Bring a Pinterest board, your color palette,
      venue photos, and an honest budget. Florals can balloon fast.
- [ ] **Book hair and makeup.** Trial typically happens 1–2 months out.
- [ ] **Order the dress** (if not already). Latest by end of December.
- [ ] **Order bridesmaid dresses / groomsmen attire** by end of January
      to allow ordering + alterations time.
- [ ] **Choose & ask your wedding party.** Make it a small moment —
      cards, a dinner, whatever. Be clear about expectations and
      approximate costs they'll incur.
- [ ] **Book transportation.** Shuttle buses for guests are highly
      recommended for foothills venues — guests should not drive
      mountain roads after drinks. Get quotes for round-trip shuttles
      from your main hotel block.
- [ ] **Book lodging for the wedding night** (often a suite at the
      venue or a nearby hotel).
- [ ] **Start the honeymoon plan.** September abroad means decisions in
      winter for flight prices. Or decide to delay the honeymoon (mini-moon
      now, real trip later) — increasingly common.
- [ ] **Register.** Now is fine. Use one site that consolidates
      (Zola, MyRegistry) so guests have one link.
- [ ] **Cake / dessert vendor.** Tastings happen ~6 months out but
      book the vendor now if they're popular.

## Phase 4 — February through April 2027 (7–5 months out): Details

- [ ] **Design invitations.** Allow 6–8 weeks for design + printing.
- [ ] **Send invitations** in **late May / early June 2027** (12–13
      weeks out for a destination-ish wedding). RSVP deadline ~5 weeks
      before the wedding.
- [ ] **Plan the rehearsal dinner.** Usually the night before
      (Friday 9/3/27). Book the restaurant or venue.
- [ ] **Welcome event** (optional but lovely for destination guests):
      casual Friday afternoon happy hour at a brewery near the hotel,
      come-and-go. Costs $20–40/person, huge ROI on guest experience.
- [ ] **Menu tasting** with the caterer.
- [ ] **Cake tasting.**
- [ ] **Hair / makeup trials.**
- [ ] **First dress fitting** (after the dress arrives).
- [ ] **Order rings.** Allow 6–8 weeks if engraving / custom.
- [ ] **Hire a month-of coordinator** if you haven't already.
- [ ] **Start the day-of timeline draft** with your coordinator.
- [ ] **Buy / order ceremony items**: signage, programs (optional),
      guestbook, card box, unity ceremony items if any.
- [ ] **Buy wedding party gifts** (give them at rehearsal dinner).

## Phase 5 — May through July 2027 (4–2 months out): Crunch

- [ ] **Send invitations** (~12 weeks out).
- [ ] **Final dress fitting #1.**
- [ ] **Vendor walkthroughs** at the venue. Bring the planner,
      photographer, florist, caterer, DJ. Get everyone on the same
      page about logistics: where the ceremony chairs go, where the
      sweetheart table sits, where the DJ plugs in, etc.
- [ ] **Finalize the music**: ceremony songs (processional, recessional,
      first kiss), reception must-plays and do-not-plays, first dance,
      parent dances.
- [ ] **Write your vows.** Block a date on the calendar two months
      out. Don't leave this for the week of.
- [ ] **Confirm rentals** (chairs, linens, lighting, dance floor, any
      tents).
- [ ] **Plan the bachelor / bachelorette parties** for July or early
      August. Don't schedule them within 2 weeks of the wedding.
- [ ] **Order welcome bags** for hotel guests (water, snacks, a
      hand-written welcome card, Tylenol & sunscreen — see CO notes).
- [ ] **Confirm transportation timing** with the shuttle company.

## Phase 6 — August 2027 (4 weeks out): Final assembly

- [ ] **Get the marriage license.** **[CO]** Colorado license is valid
      **35 days** — apply between **July 31 and Sep 4, 2027**. $30,
      no waiting period, no blood test, both parties present at the
      county clerk's office. See `05-guest-experience.md` for details.
- [ ] **Final dress fitting #2** (~2–3 weeks out).
- [ ] **Final headcount to caterer & venue** (usually due 7–10 days out).
- [ ] **Seating chart.** Lay it out in Google Sheets or AllSeated.
- [ ] **Finalize the day-of timeline** with the coordinator. Distribute
      to all vendors and the wedding party.
- [ ] **Final vendor payments** — most are due 1–2 weeks before.
- [ ] **Pack the "day-of emergency kit"**: safety pins, stain remover,
      Tide pen, extra makeup, deodorant, bandaids, painkillers, snacks,
      phone charger, sewing kit, scissors, double-stick tape, tissues,
      flat shoes.
- [ ] **Pack for the wedding day & honeymoon.**
- [ ] **Drop off welcome bags** at the hotel ~2 days before.
- [ ] **Get a haircut, manicure, etc.** ~1 week out.
- [ ] **Confirm all vendors** by phone or email 3–5 days out: arrival
      time, contact person, payment status.

## Phase 7 — Wedding week (Sep 1–4, 2027)

- **Tuesday/Wednesday:** Out-of-town family starts arriving. Light
  schedule. Get a massage if you can.
- **Thursday:** Wedding party dinner (small group, optional).
- **Friday:** Rehearsal at the venue (typically 4 PM, ~1 hour), then
  rehearsal dinner. Welcome event for all guests in the evening.
  **Go to bed early.**
- **Saturday (Sep 4 — the day):**
  - Morning: breakfast with your people, hair & makeup starts ~5 hours
    before ceremony.
  - Photos: first look, wedding party, family.
  - Ceremony.
  - Cocktail hour (you might join briefly or do more photos).
  - Reception: entrance → dinner → toasts → first dances → open dance
    floor → cake → send-off.
  - **The whole day will fly by. Schedule 10 minutes alone with your
    new spouse right after the ceremony. Everyone says it's the
    single best decision they made.**

## Phase 8 — After the wedding (September onward)

- [ ] Return rentals, attire, etc.
- [ ] Send thank-you notes (target: within 2 months).
- [ ] **[CO]** Confirm the marriage license was filed by the officiant
      with the county clerk. Order 2–3 certified copies for name
      changes, insurance, etc.
- [ ] Name change paperwork if applicable (SSA → DMV → passport → banks).
- [ ] Review photographer / videographer galleries when they arrive
      (typically 6–10 weeks post-wedding).
- [ ] Leave reviews for vendors you loved. The wedding industry runs
      on reviews and great vendors deserve it.
