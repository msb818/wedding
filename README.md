# Wedding Planning Kit

**Target date:** Saturday, September 4, 2027 (Labor Day weekend)
**Location:** Colorado foothills near Denver (Morrison / Golden / Evergreen area)
**Today's date this was started:** May 19, 2026
**Time to wedding:** ~15.5 months (~68 weeks)

This repo is your end-to-end planning workspace. Read it in order the first
time, then come back to whichever file is relevant for what you're working
on that month.

## How to use this kit

1. **Start with `01-timeline.md`** — your month-by-month checklist from now
   until the wedding day. This is the master document.
2. **`02-budget.md`** — how to set a number and split it across categories.
   Do this in the first two weeks. Everything else flows from it.
3. **`03-colorado-venues.md`** — what makes the Front Range foothills
   different (altitude, weather, fire season, Labor Day pricing) plus a
   shortlist of venues to tour and the questions to ask each one.
4. **`04-vendor-questions.md`** — the questions to ask every category of
   vendor before signing a contract. Save yourself from $5k mistakes.
5. **`05-guest-experience.md`** — lodging blocks, altitude warnings,
   transportation, welcome bags, the website. The stuff that makes guests
   feel taken care of.

## The "you know nothing" 60-second crash course

A wedding is really three things stacked together:

- **A legal event.** You sign a marriage license. In Colorado this is
  unusually easy — see `05-guest-experience.md` for details.
- **A ceremony.** ~20–30 minutes. Someone (the officiant) leads it, you say
  vows, exchange rings, kiss, walk out. That's it.
- **A party.** Cocktail hour (~1 hr), dinner (~1.5 hrs), dancing
  (~2.5–3 hrs). This is where 70–80% of your budget goes.

Around those three things you wrap: a venue, food, drinks, flowers, music,
photos, a dress and suit, invitations, and a way to get everyone there and
home. That's the entire job. Everything in this kit is just breaking those
pieces into manageable steps.

## The single most important first move

**Lock the venue.** In the Colorado foothills, Labor Day Saturday 2027 is
a premium date and the best venues are already taking inquiries 18+
months out. Until you have a venue + date confirmed in writing, you
cannot book *any* other vendor. Get this done in the next 6–8 weeks.

## The single most important mindset

You can't have everything. Pick **three things** that matter most to the
two of you (e.g. "incredible photos, great food, killer dance party") and
spend disproportionately on those. Cut hard on the rest. Couples who try
to make every detail perfect either run out of money or run out of sanity.
Usually both.

## A note on hiring a planner

For a first-time couple doing a foothills wedding with out-of-town guests,
I'd strongly recommend hiring at least a **month-of coordinator**
($2–3.5k). They take over 4–6 weeks out, run vendor logistics, and
quarterback the wedding day so you're not on your phone during cocktail
hour. **Full-service planners** ($6–15k) are worth it if your budget is
$60k+ or your jobs don't leave you time to project-manage this.

See `01-timeline.md` for when to hire which.
