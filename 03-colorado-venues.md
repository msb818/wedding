# Colorado Foothills Venues — Scouting Guide

## What's different about a foothills wedding

1. **Altitude.** Foothills venues sit between 6,500 and 8,500 feet.
   Denver is 5,280 ft. Your sea-level guests will feel it. Plan for it
   (see `05-guest-experience.md`).
2. **September weather** in the foothills, historical averages:
   - Daytime highs: 70–78°F
   - Evening lows: 42–52°F (cold once the sun drops behind the
     mountains around 7 PM)
   - Precipitation: low — Sept is one of the driest months
   - But: afternoon thunderstorms still possible into early September
   - **Action items:** plan for a 20°F temperature drop after sunset;
     have pashminas or blankets for guests at outdoor ceremonies;
     have a rain backup that you'd actually be happy with.
3. **Wildfire / smoke.** Late summer in CO can have smoke days from
   regional fires. It's unpredictable but real. Two mitigations: pick
   a venue with a great indoor backup, and don't catastrophize — most
   September weekends are clear.
4. **Labor Day weekend (Sep 4, 2027).** A Saturday on Labor Day weekend
   is one of the 3–4 highest-demand wedding dates in Colorado all year.
   Expect:
   - 10–20% pricing premium at most venues
   - Hotel rooms scarce and expensive
   - Top venues already inquiring 18+ months out
   - Upside: Monday off, so out-of-town guests can stay longer
5. **Mountain roads + drinks.** Most foothills venues require driving
   curvy mountain roads to reach. **Shuttles are non-negotiable.** Don't
   let your guests drive after drinking on Lookout Mountain Road in
   the dark.

## Sunset on 9/4/2027

Sunset in the Denver area on September 4, 2027 is approximately **7:21 PM**.
Behind the mountains, you'll lose direct sun 30–60 minutes earlier
depending on the venue's orientation.

**Practical timeline implication:** for golden-hour photos, plan your
ceremony for **4:30–5:30 PM**, cocktail hour 5:30–6:30, dinner 6:30–8:00,
dancing 8:00 onward.

## Venues worth touring (Morrison / Golden / Evergreen corridor)

This is a starting list. Use it as inspiration, not a final shortlist.
Check current availability for **Saturday, September 4, 2027**.

### Mountain view / outdoor ceremony venues

- **The Manor House** (Littleton) — historic estate with city views,
  iconic Front Range venue, gets booked very far out.
- **Mount Vernon Country Club** (Golden) — sweeping views from
  Lookout Mountain, popular with mid-large weddings.
- **Boettcher Mansion** (Golden / Lookout Mountain) — historic
  mansion, intimate, gorgeous indoor + outdoor options.
- **The Pines at Genesee** (Genesee, west of Golden) — pine forest
  setting, mountain feel, ~25 minutes from Denver.
- **Willow Ridge Manor** (Morrison) — long-running foothills venue,
  views of Mt. Falcon.
- **Arrowhead Golf Club** (Littleton) — red rock formations on the
  golf course are spectacular for ceremony.
- **Cherokee Ranch & Castle** (Sedalia, south foothills) — actual
  castle on a working ranch, dramatic.
- **Spruce Mountain Ranch** (Larkspur, ~45 min south) — multiple
  ceremony sites, accommodates large weddings.
- **Cielo at Castle Pines** (Castle Rock) — modern mountain venue
  with massive windows and a view.

### Red Rocks–adjacent

- **Red Rocks Trading Post** (Morrison) — yes, you can get married
  at Red Rocks. Limited availability, books very early, and not all
  wedding services are flexible. Worth the call if it's a dream.

### Slightly further (Boulder / Lyons / Estes Park)

- **Lionscrest Manor** (Lyons) — north of Boulder, foothills feel,
  popular.
- **Greenbriar Inn** (Boulder) — historic, smaller-scale, beautiful
  garden ceremony spot.
- **Della Terra Mountain Chateau** (Estes Park, ~1.5 hr) — full
  destination feel, all-inclusive weekend rentals. Worth a tour if
  you want a 3-day weekend vibe.

### Wedgewood Weddings properties

Wedgewood is a Colorado-based wedding venue chain known for
all-inclusive, predictable pricing. Less character than a boutique
venue but much less coordination work for you. Worth a tour as a
benchmark even if you don't book one.

- Mountain View Ranch (Pine), Black Forest, Boulder Creek, Sterling
  Hotel, etc.

## How to evaluate a venue (the tour checklist)

Bring this list to every tour. Take photos of everything. Take a
voice memo of your impressions immediately after leaving.

### Logistics
- [ ] Is **Saturday, Sep 4, 2027** available?
- [ ] What's the all-in cost including service, tax, security, parking?
      (Not just the "starting at" number.)
- [ ] What's the rental window? Setup time? Hard end time?
- [ ] How many hours of access total?
- [ ] **Noise ordinance** — when does music have to be off / inside?
      Many foothills venues have hard 10 PM outdoor music cutoffs.
- [ ] Maximum guest count? Comfortable count vs. fire-code count?
- [ ] Ceremony backup plan if it rains? Walk us through it.
- [ ] Where do guests park? Is there a shuttle from a parking lot?
- [ ] ADA accessibility for older relatives?

### What's included
- [ ] Tables, chairs, linens, dance floor, lighting?
- [ ] Sound system / mic for ceremony?
- [ ] Bridal suite / groom's suite? For how long?
- [ ] Catering — required in-house, preferred list, or fully open?
- [ ] Bar — same question. Can we bring our own alcohol? Corkage?
- [ ] Cleanup — who does it, when?

### Money
- [ ] Deposit amount and schedule?
- [ ] Final payment due date?
- [ ] Cancellation policy?
- [ ] Refund if we have to postpone due to wildfire smoke? *Critical
      question for a CO mountain wedding.*
- [ ] Liability insurance required? Most foothills venues require
      ~$1M event insurance ($150–250).

### The "feel" check
- [ ] Walk the entire space — ceremony spot, cocktail spot, reception
      spot. How do guests flow between them?
- [ ] Where do photos happen?
- [ ] What time does the sun hit the ceremony spot in early September?
      (Ask the venue or use The Photographer's Ephemeris / PhotoPills.)
- [ ] What's the view actually like — dramatic mountains, or "they
      claim mountain views but it's a parking lot"?
- [ ] Bathroom situation? (Trailer bathrooms at some venues. Fine,
      but ask.)
- [ ] Bug situation in September?
- [ ] Are the bridal / groom suites actually usable spaces, or a
      converted closet?

## A note on "all-inclusive" venues

Some venues (Wedgewood, some country clubs) bundle catering, bar,
DJ, cake, florals. They're 20–30% cheaper than à la carte and 10x
less work. The tradeoff is less customization — your wedding will
look and feel similar to others at the same venue. If your "top 3"
priorities are not about uniqueness, all-inclusive is a smart play
and not a compromise.

## Booking sequence

1. Make a list of 8–10 venues from above + your own research.
2. Email all of them with: target date, guest count, budget ballpark,
   ask for availability and pricing.
3. Knock the list down to 4–6 based on responses.
4. Tour those 4–6 over 2–3 weekends. Always tour together.
5. Decide within a week of the last tour. Re-touring "the one or
   two finalists" is fine and recommended.
6. Read the contract twice. Ask a lawyer friend if anything is
   unclear. Sign and pay the deposit.

**Target: signed venue contract by end of July 2026.**
