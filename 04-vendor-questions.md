# Vendor Questions Cheat Sheet

Use the relevant section when interviewing each vendor type. The goal of
these questions is to avoid the **three vendor disasters** that ruin
weddings:

1. They don't show up (or send a B-team substitute).
2. They drastically under-deliver vs. what you thought you bought.
3. They cost way more than the contract suggested.

Every question below exists to prevent one of those.

## Universal questions (ask every vendor)

- Are you available on **Saturday, Sep 4, 2027**?
- Are you the person who will personally work my wedding? If not, who
  will, and can I meet them before signing?
- What's your backup plan if you're sick or in an accident the week
  of the wedding?
- How many weddings do you book per weekend? (For a Labor Day Saturday,
  most photographers/DJs/etc. should be doing only one event.)
- What's your deposit and payment schedule?
- What's your cancellation / postponement policy?
- Are you licensed and insured? (Most venues require vendor COIs —
  certificates of insurance.)
- Have you worked at [our venue] before? What should we know about it?
- Can you share 2–3 recent references I can call?
- What's the contract turnaround? Can we review before signing?

## Photographer

- May I see a complete recent wedding gallery (not a highlight reel)?
  Especially one shot in conditions like ours — early evening light,
  outdoor ceremony, similar guest count.
- How do you handle the ~6:30–7:30 PM "golden to dim" transition in
  Colorado in September? Strobes? Reflectors? Available light?
- What's the deliverable: number of edited images, expected delivery
  timeline, file format, online gallery, print rights?
- Do we get the RAW files? (Most say no — that's normal, not a
  red flag.)
- Second shooter included? Recommended?
- Albums / prints — included or upsell? At what price?
- How many hours of coverage are included? Overtime rate?
- Travel fees for foothills venue?
- Do you allow guest photos / unplugged ceremony — what's your stance?

## Videographer

- May I see a full ceremony + reception film, not just a 90-second
  highlight reel?
- Deliverables: highlight film length, full ceremony, full toasts,
  raw footage?
- Drone footage? (Note: drone use is restricted in many foothills
  areas — verify with venue.)
- Audio capture — how many mics, how are toasts and vows recorded?
- Delivery timeline (12 weeks is normal; 6 months is too long).

## Caterer

- Bring a menu and a head count to the first conversation.
- Cost per person fully loaded — food, service, rentals, tax, gratuity?
- What's the staffing ratio? (Industry standard: 1 server per
  10–15 plated guests.)
- Do you do dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, GF, allergies)?
- Tasting — how many people invited, when in the year, cost?
- Bar service — included or separate?
- **[CO altitude question]** Have you cooked at this venue's altitude?
  Some baked goods and sauces behave differently above 7,000 ft.
- Cake cutting fee? (Often $1–3/guest if you bring in an outside cake.)
- Leftovers policy — can we take food home / donate?
- Vendor meals included? (You're obligated to feed your photographer,
  DJ, planner, etc. ~$25–50/vendor meal.)

## Bar

- Can we BYO alcohol or must we buy through the venue/caterer?
- Bartender ratio (1 per 75 guests minimum, 1 per 50 ideal).
- Hours of service. Last call timing.
- Signature cocktail option? Cost?
- Non-alcoholic options — do they have actual good NA options or just
  Sprite and water?
- Corkage fee if BYO wine?
- Insurance / liquor liability — who carries it?

## Florist

- Bring: Pinterest board, color palette, venue photos, dress photo (if
  ordered), budget number you're comfortable sharing.
- What's in season in Colorado in early September?
- Itemized quote — bouquets, boutonnieres, centerpieces, ceremony
  florals, anything else?
- Setup and breakdown — included or extra?
- What happens to the florals after — can guests take centerpieces?
- Allergies / pet considerations?
- Backup plan if a key flower isn't available?

## DJ

- Will you be the actual DJ at our wedding? Can we see you DJ live or
  watch video of you?
- MC style — energetic, low-key, somewhere in between?
- How do you handle the do-play / do-not-play lists?
- How do you read a room?
- Equipment included — ceremony sound system, wireless mics for
  officiant + toasts, dance lighting?
- Backup equipment on-site?
- Overtime rate?

## Band

- All the DJ questions, plus:
- Will every member of the band on the demo also play at our wedding?
- How long are sets? How do breaks work? (Playlist between sets, or
  silence?)
- Genres / song requests — how many can we request?
- Power needs — coordinate with venue.
- Space needs (square footage on stage, ceiling height).
- Do they MC and do announcements, or do you need a separate MC?

## Officiant

- Have you officiated weddings before? Can we see / hear one?
- Style — religious, spiritual, secular, comedic, somber?
- Will you co-write the ceremony with us?
- How long is the typical ceremony? (Sweet spot is 18–25 minutes.)
- Are you legally able to officiate in Colorado? (Pretty much anyone
  can — see `05-guest-experience.md`.)
- Will you handle the marriage license filing afterward?

## Planner / coordinator

- What level of service — full, partial, month-of?
- How many weddings do you take per month? Per year?
- Will you be the lead coordinator on our day, or will you assign us
  to someone on your team?
- How many planners on-site the day of?
- What's the timeline of involvement — when do you start working
  with us?
- Do you have a preferred vendor list? Are we required to use it?
- Tools / process — Aisle Planner, Honeybook, Google Drive?
- What does "day of" actually mean — how many hours? When do you
  arrive and leave?
- Walk us through a sample day-of timeline you've executed.

## Hair & makeup

- On-site at the venue or your studio?
- Trial included? Cost if not?
- How long per person? How many artists on the team?
- Touch-up kit included?
- Airbrush vs. traditional makeup?
- Lashes / extensions experience?
- What products do you use (relevant if you have sensitive skin)?

## Transportation

- Vehicle types and capacity?
- Round-trip pricing — hotel to venue and back?
- Multiple runs? Continuous loop service?
- Insurance and licensing (any reputable company will share).
- Last call / pickup timing?
- What happens if a guest is too drunk to be on the shuttle? (Real
  scenario.)

## Red flags across all vendor types

- Won't share recent full work samples.
- Pressure tactics ("this date is going fast, you need to sign today").
- Vague pricing — refuses to provide an itemized quote.
- Bad communication during the sales process. *It only gets worse
  after the deposit.*
- No contract or a one-page contract with no specifics.
- All reviews are 5 stars from new accounts (suspicious) — or no
  reviews at all on Google / The Knot / WeddingWire.
- Won't share references.
- Asks for full payment up front, or 50%+ deposit on first signing.
