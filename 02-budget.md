# Budget Framework

## Step 1: Pick a total number

Before you allocate anything, agree on one total dollar figure including
everything (rings, dress, honeymoon, the whole thing). Have an explicit
conversation about:

- How much each of you can contribute from savings.
- How much you're each comfortable adding from cash flow over the next 15
  months.
- Whether either family is contributing — and if so, **get a number, not
  a feeling.** "Whatever you need" is not a number. Ask them politely
  but directly: "It would help us plan to know roughly what amount you're
  comfortable with." Then add it (or 80% of it, to be safe) to the pot.
- A 10% contingency line. You will spend it. Everyone does.

## Denver / Front Range cost realities (2026–2027)

These are rough averages for a ~100-person foothills wedding. Yours will
vary. Numbers trend high because Labor Day weekend is peak season.

| Category | Budget range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Venue** (site fee only) | $4k–$15k | Foothills venues with views: $8–15k for Saturday peak |
| **Catering** (food + staff) | $90–$200 / guest | Includes service. Plated > buffet > stations on cost |
| **Bar** (alcohol + bartender) | $30–$80 / guest | BYO alcohol can save 30–50% if venue allows |
| **Photographer** | $4k–$8k | 8-hour coverage. Top-tier: $9–14k |
| **Videographer** | $3k–$6k | Optional but high regret rate if skipped |
| **Florals** | $4k–$10k | Bouquets + centerpieces + ceremony. Easy to overspend |
| **DJ** | $2k–$3.5k | Includes ceremony sound + MC |
| **Band** | $5k–$12k | 4–6 piece. Big upgrade in dance floor energy |
| **Cake / desserts** | $500–$2k | Or do a dessert bar / pies / donuts for less |
| **Stationery** (save-the-date + invites + day-of signage) | $800–$2.5k | DIY can drop this to $300 |
| **Hair & makeup** (you + 4 bridesmaids) | $1k–$2.5k | $200–$400 per person |
| **Wedding dress + alterations** | $1.5k–$5k | Sample sales and Stillwhite cut this in half |
| **Suit / tux** | $300–$1500 | Off-the-rack to MTM |
| **Rings** (both) | $1k–$5k | Wide range. Lab-grown diamonds = same look, ~1/3 price |
| **Officiant** | $0–$1k | Free if friend; $500–1000 for pro. $0 for [CO] self-solemnization |
| **Transportation** (shuttles) | $1k–$3k | Strongly recommended for foothills |
| **Rentals** (chairs, linens, lighting) | $1k–$8k | Depends what venue includes |
| **Planner / coordinator** | $2k–$15k | Month-of: $2–3.5k. Partial: $4–7k. Full: $7–15k |
| **Honeymoon** | $3k–$15k+ | Budget separately if possible |
| **Marriage license** | $30 | [CO] flat fee |
| **Tips for vendors** | $500–$1500 | Plan for it; cash in envelopes ready at rehearsal |
| **Contingency** | 10% of total | You will use it |

## Sample budgets

### "Tight" — $30k, 70 guests, weekday or off-season feel

- Mid-range venue with most rentals included ($4k)
- Buffet catering at $90/guest ($6.3k)
- Limited bar / beer + wine only ($25/guest = $1.75k)
- Solid local photographer, 6 hours ($3.5k)
- DJ ($2.2k)
- DIY florals or focus on greenery + candles ($2k)
- Off-the-rack dress + simple suit ($1.5k)
- Month-of coordinator ($2.5k)
- Everything else ($3k)
- Contingency ($3k)

### "Average Denver foothills" — $55k, 100 guests

- Mid–high venue ($9k)
- Plated catering at $135/guest ($13.5k)
- Full bar at $50/guest ($5k)
- Top-half photographer ($6k)
- Videographer ($4k)
- DJ ($2.8k)
- Florals ($6k)
- Dress + suit + alterations ($3.5k)
- Month-of coordinator ($3k)
- Everything else ($6k)
- Contingency ($5k)

### "Splurge" — $90k+, 120 guests

- Top-tier view venue ($14k)
- Plated catering with upgrades at $180/guest ($21.6k)
- Premium open bar ($75/guest = $9k)
- Top-tier photographer + videographer ($14k)
- 6-piece band ($9k)
- Statement florals ($10k)
- Partial-service planner ($6k)
- Everything else ($8k)
- Contingency ($8k)

## Rules of thumb for cutting cost without anyone noticing

1. **Cut the guest count.** $150/guest x 20 people = $3k. The single
   highest-leverage lever. Be ruthless: if you wouldn't grab dinner
   alone with them this year, they don't need to be there.
2. **Sunday or Friday wedding.** 10–25% off most vendors. Note: Labor
   Day weekend has Monday off, so Sunday Sep 5 is actually a great
   option if a venue offers a discount.
3. **Beer + wine + signature cocktail** instead of full open bar.
   Nobody will notice.
4. **Skip the favors.** Nobody takes them home.
5. **Drop paper save-the-dates,** email or website only.
6. **Greenery + candles** instead of large floral arrangements.
7. **DJ instead of band.** $5k+ saved, dance floor still great with
   a good DJ.
8. **Cake-cutting cake + sheet cake** in the back, instead of one
   giant designed cake.
9. **Family-style or buffet** instead of plated. Often $20+/guest less
   and lots of people prefer it anyway.
10. **Sample sale or pre-owned dress.** Stillwhite, Nearly Newlywed,
    sample sale events at Denver bridal shops.

## Track everything

Make a Google Sheet with columns: Category, Vendor, Estimate, Contract
amount, Deposit paid, Final due date, Final amount paid. Update after
every contract. This alone prevents 90% of "how did we spend so much"
surprises.
